## 摘要
工业推荐系统通常以召回、粗排和精排的级联方式运行，但这些阶段通常被训练和部署为独立模型，导致用户序列重复编码、优化相互隔离以及工程重复投入。在 OneTrans 的模型级统一基础上，我们提出 OneTrans-V2，用一个 Transformer 统一整个级联。它将用户行为序列一次性编码为共享上下文，同时保留各阶段特有的候选特征与计算。联合训练使三个阶段能够相互增强，并支持从精排到粗排的模型内知识蒸馏。我们使用稀疏专家混合（MoE）扩展共享骨干，从而在有界激活计算下提升容量，并通过 μP 风格参数化稳定扩展。为整合面向不同目标的召回通道，我们提出决策条件生成式召回（Decision-Conditioned Generative Retrieval, DCGR）。DCGR 预测描述即将发生交互的决策前缀，并以其为条件生成物品，从而让业务目标引导单一的生成过程。最后，序列原生训练（Sequence-Native Training, SNT）围绕每个用户的终身行为序列组织训练，并将其编码在多次曝光间摊销。OneTrans-V2 已部署于一个大规模工业推荐系统的全部三个阶段，将商品交易总额（GMV）提升 9.74%，并在协同设计的服务栈下，在相同硬件预算下实现其替代的级联系统 3.2 倍的吞吐量。

## Abstract
Industrial recommendation systems typically operate as a \emph{cascade} of retrieval, pre-rank, and fine-rank, but these stages are usually trained and served as separate models, causing repeated user-sequence encoding, isolated optimization, and duplicated engineering effort. Building on OneTrans' model-level unification, we present OneTrans-V2, one Transformer that unifies the entire cascade. It encodes the user behavior sequence once as a shared context while preserving stage-specific candidate features and computation. Joint training lets the three stages reinforce one another and enables in-model knowledge distillation from fine-rank to pre-rank. We scale the shared backbone with sparse mixture-of-experts (MoE), which increases capacity with bounded activated computation, and stabilize scaling with $μ$P-style parameterization. To consolidate objective-specific retrieval channels, we introduce Decision-Conditioned Generative Retrieval (DCGR). DCGR predicts a decision prefix describing the upcoming interaction and generates items conditioned on it, allowing business objectives to steer a single generative process. Finally, Sequence-Native Training (SNT) organizes training around each user's lifelong behavior sequence and amortizes its encoding across exposures. Deployed across all three stages of a large-scale industrial recommendation system, OneTrans-V2 improves gross merchandise value (GMV) by 9.74\% and, with a co-designed serving stack, delivers $3.2\times$ the throughput of the cascade it replaces under the same hardware budget.
